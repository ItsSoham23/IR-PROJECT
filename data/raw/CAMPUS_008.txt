🎓 🌏🎓 GLOBAL PhD OPPORTUNITY | BITS Pilani × RMIT University🎓🌏🎉

Two Universities. One Degree. A Global Research Journey.The Department of Civil Engineering, BITS Pilani, Pilani Campus, is pleased to share an exciting joint PhD opportunity with RMIT University, Australia.Are you passionate about Civil Engineering, Materials Science, Manufacturing, Computer Science, or related fields?Applications are invited for a PhD project on:🔬 Programmable SmartCrete: AI-Optimized Sustainable Binders with Digital Twin IntegrationThe project focuses on developing a new generation of AI-designed smart cementitious composites that are low-carbon, multifunctional, and digitally integrated. ✨ What does this opportunity offer?🎓 Joint PhD award from BITS Pilani and RMIT University💰 Full tuition scholarship at RMIT and highly subsidised fees at BITS💵 Stipend, health cover and return airfare while based in Melbourne🤝 Co-supervision by faculty from both institutions🔬 Access to world-class research infrastructure🇮🇳🇦🇺 A global academic experience, beginning at BITS Pilani and extending to up to one year in Australia 📌 Project DetailsProject ID: BITS025F001518Project Title:Programmable SmartCrete: AI-Optimized Sustainable Binders with Digital Twin Integration🔗 Project Details:https://lnkd.in/gqv4Z-fP📝 Apply Now⏰ Application Deadline: 10 October 2026👉 Application Portal:https://lnkd.in/gcGyVQ4F📧 For further information:mukund.lahoti@pilani.bits-pilani.ac.in 🌟 Know someone who would be interested in this opportunity?
