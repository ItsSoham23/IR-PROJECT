🎓 🌏 GSIA 2027 | Global Symposium on Infrastructure for Atmanirbhar India🌏🎉

We are pleased to inform you about GSIA 2027 – Global Symposium on Infrastructure for Atmanirbhar India, being organised by the Department of Civil Engineering, BITS Pilani – Pilani Campus.🏗️ Infrastructure for Atmanirbhar India: A BITS Pilani Initiative for Viksit Bharat📅 19–20 February 2027📍 BITS Pilani – Pilani Campus, RajasthanThe symposium aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, practitioners, policymakers and innovators to translate research into practice and foster meaningful industry–academia collaboration for sustainable, resilient and future-ready infrastructure. BITS Pilani🔬 Four Key Symposium ThemesSmart & Structural Infrastructure EngineeringSustainable Construction, Materials & Geotechnical EngineeringWater Systems, Environment & Climate ResilienceGeospatial Technologies, Transportation & Urban Innovation BITS Pilani📢 Call for PapersAuthors can contribute through:Track A: Abstract + Poster PresentationTrack B: Abstract + Full-Length PaperAccepted contributions will be considered for publication through Springer Nature Proceedings. BITS Pilani📌 Important Dates:Abstract Submission: 1 November 2026Acceptance Notification: 15 November 2026Full-Length Paper Submission: 15 December 2026Symposium: 19–20 February 2027 BITS Pilani🔗 For symposium details, submission guidelines, registration and important dates, visit:https://lnkd.in/e5AQhnX5Join us in shaping the future of sustainable, resilient and self-reliant infrastructure for Viksit Bharat. 🇮🇳hashtag#GSIA2027 hashtag#BITSPilani hashtag#CivilEngineering hashtag#Infrastructure hashtag#AtmanirbharIndia hashtag#ViksitBharat hashtag#SustainableInfrastructure hashtag#Research hashtag#Innovation hashtag#AcademiaIndustry hashtag#CallForPapers hashtag#SpringerNature
