🎉 Day 3 of ASMMI 2025 Wraps Up with Expert Talks, Dynamic Panel Discussion & Grand Valedictory! 🎓

🏗️ Session on Sustainable Infrastructure 🌍

The final day of ASMMI 2025 was packed with thought-provoking discussions, expert insights, and a grand closing ceremony—marking the successful conclusion of this premier event!

🎤 Plenary Lecture by Prof. Yang En-Hua 🎓

🔹 The day kicked off with an insightful plenary lecture by Prof. Yang En-Hua from Nanyang Technological University, Singapore 🌏.🔹 Delivering his session virtually, he spoke on:

💡 "Strategic Utilization of Municipal Solid Waste Incinerator Bottom Ash as Construction Material via Systematic Classification and Characterization." ♻️🏗️

The session set the tone for an innovative and knowledge-packed day ahead! 🚀

🎯 Parallel Technical Sessions – Experts Take the Stage!

Following the plenary, three parallel technical sessions unfolded, featuring some of the most esteemed speakers:

✅ Mr. Rajiv Goel (Earthcon Systems Pvt. Ltd.) – Case Study on SMSE Wall at Khellani Tunnel Approval Portals 🏗️📊✅ Col. Naresh Sharma (Indian Army Veteran, Infrastructure Expert) – "Common Mistakes by Consultants in Planning of Sewer Networks and STPs." 🚰🚧✅ Mr. Vanjul Jain (MD & CEO, Youngman) – Shared key insights on modern infrastructure solutions 🏢

Each session was interactive, informative, and impactful! 🎯

🔥 Panel Discussion: Bridging the Gap Between Academia & Industry 🔥

One of the most awaited sessions of ASMMI 2025 was the panel discussion on:

💡 "Bridging the Gap between Academia and Industry in Sustainable and Smart Infrastructure Development." 🌱🏗️

The esteemed panelists included:

👨‍🏫 Prof. Rajiv Gupta (BITS Pilani)👨‍🏫 Prof. A. P. Singh (BITS Pilani)👨‍🎓 Col. Soumyabrata Chakraborty (BITS Pilani)🏗️ Mr. Rajiv Goel (Earthcon Systems)🏗️ Col. (Mr.) Naresh Sharma (Infrastructure Expert)🌍 Prof. Phalguni Mukhopadhyaya (University of Victoria, Canada)

✨ Key Takeaways from the Discussion:

🔹 Strengthening industry-academia collaboration 🤝🔹 Encouraging innovative sustainable infrastructure solutions 🌍🔹 Fostering practical skill development for students & professionals 📚

The discussion was engaging, solution-driven, and highly relevant to today's infrastructure challenges! 🚧💡

🏆 A Grand Closing – Valedictory Ceremony 🎓

The conference concluded on a high note with a special valedictory session, where the Guest of Honour 🎖️ Mr. Rajiv Goel (Earthcon Systems Pvt. Ltd.) and Col. (Mr.) Naresh Sharma were felicitated for their remarkable contributions.

Both delivered inspiring closing addresses, sharing their vision for the future of sustainable infrastructure development 🌍🏗️.

🙌 A Heartfelt Vote of Thanks

🎤 Prof. Brij Kishore Pandey, Organizing Chairman, BITS Pilani Dubai Campus, delivered a Vote of Thanks, expressing:

💙 Gratitude to all participants, speakers, and organizers 🙏🏆 Appreciation for the efforts in making ASMMI 2025 a grand success 🎉

🔥 ASMMI 2025 wrapped up as a memorable, impactful, and transformative event – paving the way for sustainable innovations in infrastructure! 🚀💡
