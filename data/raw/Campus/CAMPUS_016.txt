This workshop, unique of its kind, was conducted by the Department of Civil Engineering, BITS Pilani, Pilani campus on the 1st February 2025 at VFast in two phases. Phase I was a rigorous Group discussion (three groups were created) among faculty and research scholars to explore opportunities on interdisciplinary research. Phase II involved the Placement cell team discussing placement scenarios with UG and PG students and providing valuable inputs for further betterment of the placement especially in the core civil engineering domain. Thanks to Prof. Dipendu Bhunia, all faculty members, the Placement team, research scholars, and UG and PG students.
