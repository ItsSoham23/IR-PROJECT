🎓 🌏 PhD Admission Opening-  BIRLA INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY & SCIENCE, PILANI, Pilani Campus | An Institution of Eminence 🌏🎉

APPLICATION DEADLINE  •  06 NOVEMBER 2026

ELIGIBILITY

E./ M.Tech. /M.Sc. or equivalent degree with at least 60% aggregate marks in the relevant field.

Preference will be given to GATE/NET qualified candidates.

FINANCIAL ASSISTANCE

Institute fellowship: ₹37,000 per month.

30% HRA for students residing outside the BITS campus.

Annual research contingency grant: ₹20,000.

SELECTION PROCESS

Only eligible candidates will be shortlisted for a written test followed by an in-person interview.

Written test and Interview: 21 November 2026

APPLY ONLINE
