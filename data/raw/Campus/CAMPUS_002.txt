PhD Scholar Opportunity at BITS Pilani 🚨(Date Extended to 15th Oct,2026 -Time -5:00 PM) 📍 Pilani Campus | Immediate Joining🔬 Join an exciting cross-disciplinary research project on:🧪 Self-Healing Cementitious Composites for Mechanical Engineering Applications📌 Project Title:Computationally Guided Design of Bacteria-Induced Self-Healing Engineered Cementitious Composites (ECC)👨‍🏫 Supervisors:• Prof. Amit Singh• Prof. Mukund Lahoti✨ Research Areas:✔ Strain-hardening ECC materials✔ Fiber–matrix mechanical behavior✔ Bacteria-based self-healing mechanisms✔ AI & multiscale predictive modeling🎓 Eligibility:B.E./B.Tech./M.E./M.Tech. in:• Mechanical Engineering• Civil Engineering• Or related disciplines💰 Fellowship:₹37,000 – ₹42,000 per month(as per BITS Pilani norms)📅 Application Deadline:15th Oct,2026 (Time : 5 PM)📩 Apply by sending your CV to:amit.singh@pilani.bits-pilani.ac.inmukund.lahoti@pilani.bits-pilani.ac.in🧠 Selection Process:Shortlisting → Online Interview → Final Selection🚀 Ideal for candidates passionate about:Advanced materials | Sustainable infrastructure | AI in engineering | Smart compositeshashtag#PhDOpportunity hashtag#BITSPilani hashtag#Research hashtag#CivilEngineering hashtag#MechanicalEngineering hashtag#SelfHealingConcrete hashtag#ECC hashtag#ArtificialIntelligence hashtag#SmartMaterials
