Soft Matter Meet – (Hyderabad Campus)

Hyderabad Soft Matter Meet (HSMM) 2026 took place at BITS-Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, on September 1, 2026, organized under the umbrella of SIRE. The one-day symposium was full of insightful talks, poster presentations, and discussions spanning a wide range of topics, viz., statistical mechanics, fluid dynamics, biophysics, microfluidics, liquid crystal, glass, etc. Faculty members, Postdoctoral Researchers, Ph.D scholars and Undergraduate Students from  institutions across Hyderabad participated and presented their work. The symposium created a platform for interaction among researchers from different academic disciplines to exchange ideas, discuss and find future collaborators.

Research Paper & Proposal Writing / Academic Plagiarism and Ethics – (Pilani Campus)

The School of Interdisciplinary Research and Entrepreneurship (SIRE BITS Pilani) and the Department of Mechanical Engineering BITS Pilani, Pilani Campus successfully organized an insightful invited talk by Prof. B.N. Basu (Professor (Adjunct), Sir JC Bose School of Engineering, SKFGI, Mankundu, West Bengal, Ex-Professor and Head, Electronics Engineering Department, and Ex-Coordinator, Centre of Research in Microwave Tubes, Institute of Technology, Banaras Hindu University (now known as IIT-BHU) ) on 1st April 2026. Participants gained practical insights and engaged in meaningful discussions throughout the session. We sincerely thank Prof. Basu for sharing his expertise and all participants for their enthusiastic presence.

Mandatory 3-Day PhD DRIVE Workshop – (Hyderabad Campus)

To support doctoral scholars under the PhD DRIVE (Deep-tech Research, Innovation, Value generation & Entrepreneurship) program, the School of Interdisciplinary Research and Entrepreneurship at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus (SIRE-BPHC) allocated dedicated travel grants to reimburse travel expenses for its research cohort. This financial support enabled PhD DRIVE scholars to travel from the Hyderabad campus to the BITS Pilani main campus to attend a mandatory three-day workshop held from March 16 to 18, 2026. By removing travel barriers, SIRE ensured seamless multi-campus integration, allowing doctoral candidates to fully engage in the intensive training, cross-campus mentorship, and collaborative deep-tech innovation sessions planned at the Pilani campus.

PhD DRIVE Program Workshop – Deep-Tech Research to Startup – (Pilani Campus)

The Ph.D. DRIVE Program Workshop, jointly organized by the School of Interdisciplinary Research & Entrepreneurship (SIRE) and the Pilani Innovation and Entrepreneurship Development Society (PIEDS) at the Rakesh Kapoor Innovation Centre (RKIC), was a two-day intensive initiative aimed at facilitating the translation of deep-tech academic research into viable commercial ventures. The workshop was inaugurated by Hon’ble Vice-Chancellor, Prof. V. Ramgopal Rao, BITS Pilani, and featured expert-led sessions on key aspects of research commercialization, including intellectual property and patent filing strategies, prototyping and product development, medical device regulatory requirements, and investor readiness. The workshop culminated in pitch presentations by 16 Ph.D. DRIVE scholars from the Pilani, Hyderabad, and Goa campuses of BITS Pilani -led startups before investor panels, providing valuable opportunities for expert feedback, industry networking, and potential funding.

Research Scholars’ Day – Poster Presentations & Evaluation – (Pilani Campus)

Research Scholars’ Day, organized by the School of Interdisciplinary Research & Entrepreneurship (SIRE) and held on National Science Day at VFAST Hall, brought together Ph.D. interdisciplinary scholars, Ph.D. DRIVE scholars, faculty supervisors and co- supervisors, and Departmental Advisory Committee (DAC) members. The event provided a platform for Ph.D. DRIVE scholars and interdisciplinary Ph.D. scholars to present their research work through interactive poster presentations. The presentations were evaluated by an expert committee based on technical novelty, real-world applicability, and commercial potential. The event concluded with the recognition of the top three research presentations, encouraging high-impact interdisciplinary research, innovation, and the translation of research outcomes into startups and other real-world applications.
