SSX-2027 | Decennial Conference of SSX-IndiaA Decade of Advancing Drug Discovery and Development: From Data to DecisionsWe are pleased to announce SSX-2027, the 10th/Decennial Conference of the Society for the Study of Xenobiotics (SSX-India), to be held at BITS Pilani, K K Birla Goa Campus from 27–29 January 2027.

Conference Theme“Zooming into the Future of Drug Discovery amp; Development: Convergence of AI, Human Biology, and Translational DMPK”The conference will bring together academicians, researchers, students, industry professionals, and scientific experts to discuss emerging advances and innovations across:

Date: 27–29 January 2027

Venue: BITS Pilani, K K Birla Goa Campus

Registration Fee: Inclusive of GST For Students: Bonafide Certificate is mandatory

Register for SSX-2027:https://lnkd.in/dpK-q3n4

Conference Website:https://ssxindia.com/

Why Attend?

A great opportunity to: Learn from leading experts in drug discovery and development Explore the convergence of AI, human biology, and translational DMPK Exchange ideas and research findings Build academic and industry collaborations Network with researchers, students, and professionals Contribute to the future of pharmaceutical and biomedical sciences

Join us in Goa for three days of scientific exchange, collaboration, innovation, and discovery!We look forward to welcoming the scientific community to BITS Pilani, K K Birla Goa Campus for SSX-2027.
