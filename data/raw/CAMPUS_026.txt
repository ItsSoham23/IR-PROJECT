🎉🚧🌟 Workshop & Research Scholars’ Day – Highlights Department of Civil Engineering, BITS Pilani | 22 Nov 2025🎉

The Civil Engineering Department hosted an inspiring Workshop & Research Scholars’ Day focusing on interdisciplinary research, innovation, and career readiness. The event brought together scholars, faculty, and experts for meaningful discussions on strengthening our research ecosystem.🔬 Key Takeaways• Strong need for advanced lab upgrades—Impact Testing Machine, LS-DYNA, improved geotechnical tools, auto-samplers, and modern sensors.• Regular training for XRF, SEM, TGA, DSC to enhance confidence and ensure responsible instrument usage.• Emphasis on building high-end computational facilities and AI-ready research labs.• Proposal for intensive AI/ML training workshops to elevate researcher capabilities across domains.• Scholars highlighted the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration—from water–agriculture models to traffic–pavement interactions and integrated structural–environmental studies.• Strengthening the Research Forum for active knowledge sharing, presentations, and methodological discussions.• Need for proposal-writing training and an institute-level idea-exchange platform.🏅 Poster AwardsCongratulations to Research Scholars HARISHVAR J., 𝐓𝐚𝐬𝐚𝐝𝐮𝐪 𝐈𝐬𝐦𝐚𝐢𝐥 𝐖𝐚𝐧𝐢 , and RAKESH KUMAR V G for winning the Best Poster Awards, presented by Prof. Sudhirkumar Barai, Director, Bits-Pilani, Pilani Campus🙏 AcknowledgementsGratitude toProf. Sudhirkumar V Barai Director, BITS Pilani, Pilani CampusCol. Soumyabrata Chakraborty ,Registrar, BITS Pilani,Prof. Dipendu Bhunia (Ph.D, Aff.M ASCE, L.M.ICI) (HOD), Department Of Civil Engineering, BITS-Pilani, Pilani CampusProf Srinivas Rallapalli (Organizing Committee)Prof Pubali Mandal (Organizing Committee)Faculty members & all research scholarsfor making this event a grand success.The department remains committed to advancing an innovative, collaborative, and future-ready research culture.
